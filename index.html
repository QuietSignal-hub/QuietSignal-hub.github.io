About

I started in operations and supply chain, then went deep on statistics and modeling. Most of my work sits between the two: taking messy operational data, getting it right, and turning it into something a team can plan around.

That has meant forecasting daily demand for staffing decisions, mapping alternate shipping routes when a canal becomes a bottleneck, building a SQL data warehouse so reporting stops double-counting, and segmenting 24,000+ grant recipients so an outreach team knows where to start.

I care about the step most people skip: checking the data before trusting the result. On more than one project the most useful thing I found was a bug in the data, not a pattern in it.

October 2025, graduate project

Predictive Modeling of Stop Outcomes, Saint Paul, MN

Compared four classifiers on 675K records and handled class imbalance so the model caught the rare outcomes, not just the common ones.

ROC-AUC 0.943, minority-class recall 0.77

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • SMOTE
How I built it
  • Cleaned 675,156 records and engineered time and demographic features in pandas and NumPy.
  • Built and compared logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, and a stacked ensemble (MLP and XGBoost with a logistic regression meta-learner).
  • Used SMOTE for class imbalance, reaching 0.945 accuracy and ROC-AUC 0.943 with minority-class recall of 0.77.
  • Explained model behavior with feature importance and confusion matrices in Matplotlib and Seaborn.
