About

I started in operations and supply chain, then went deep on statistics and modeling. Most of my work sits between the two: taking messy operational data, getting it right, and turning it into something a team can plan around.

That has meant forecasting daily demand for staffing decisions, mapping alternate shipping routes and measuring the trade impact of the Suez Canal blockage, building a SQL data warehouse so reporting stops double-counting, and segmenting 24,000+ grant recipients so an outreach team knows where to start.

On more than one project, the most useful thing I found was a bug in the data, not a pattern in it. Checking the data before trusting the result is the habit I bring to every team.
